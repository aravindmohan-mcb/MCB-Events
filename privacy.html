Privacy Policy

This site · Back to events

This site is used to register for training programs, workshops and events organised by Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

In your profile and registrations you may also provide details such as your mobile number, gender, date of birth, qualification, institution, location, category, an ID proof number, a photo, and, for paid events, a payment reference or screenshot.

How we use it

Your information is used only to manage your registration for events, to contact you about events you registered for, to issue certificates, and to prepare participation reports that the organisers must submit to the funding and government bodies that support these programs, such as the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a private Google Sheet and a private Google Drive folder owned by the organisers. Only the event administrators can view it. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Participation details may be shared with the funding or government agency for the program you joined, as needed for official reporting.

Emails

We send emails only about events you have registered for, and only for events where the organisers have turned confirmations on.

Your choices

You can view and update your profile on this site at any time. To ask for your data to be corrected or removed, contact us at the address below.
